# Product: down.

**One line:** Swipe on plans before they exist. Back the ones you want to happen.

down. is a Boston events app where people discover and back *proposed* plans. A plan only becomes a real event when enough people say they're down. It combines three familiar patterns:

| Pattern | What we borrow |
|---|---|
| Tinder | A swipeable card deck: right = "I'm down", left = "nah" |
| Kickstarter | A goal per plan and all-or-nothing pledges (nobody is charged unless it happens) |
| Luma / Eventbrite | The event page, ticket with QR, wallet, reminders |

## Mission

Create more in-person gatherings in Boston so people connect regardless of who they already know. Get young people into neighborhoods they don't usually visit, meeting people they wouldn't otherwise meet. Give hosts real earning potential. Push back on brain drain: people stay in cities where they have their people and their places.

## Who it serves (three pathways)

- **Public:** cities, libraries, parks departments, nonprofits. Proof of demand before spending public money, and a "new to this neighborhood" impact metric.
- **Private:** brands and small businesses. Test a pop-up with zero risk; real market validation.
- **Personal:** friends, group chats, classrooms. "We should..." becomes a date, a place and a crowd.

## The core unit: a plan

Every plan moves through four stages, shown everywhere a plan appears:

`Pitched` → `Heating up` (≥ 50% of goal) → `It's on` (goal hit) → `Happened`

Commitment ladder:
- **Free plans** fill an interest bar to the goal. Spots become tickets when it's on.
- **Paid plans** hold a card (early-bird) and charge only if the goal is hit. Otherwise nothing happens and nobody pays.

## Product principles

1. **Nobody pays for a plan that doesn't happen.** Copy must always make this clear at the moment of commitment.
2. **Every plan is somewhere new.** Surface "N people backing this have never been to <neighborhood>" and stamp a Passport when you show up.
3. **The goal moment is the product.** Hitting the goal ("It's on.") must feel like a celebration: full-screen, confetti, ticket.
4. **Anyone can host.** Pitching a plan takes four short steps and starts in your group chat.
5. **Boston-specific, human copy.** Real neighborhoods, real transit lines ("Silver Line · Nubian"), short warm sentences, lowercase wordmark `down.`.

## The demo moment (must work exactly)

1. "Salsa under the lights" is the first card at **148/150**.
2. Swipe right (or tap I'm down) → **149**, toast "You're down. 1 more and it happens".
3. 1.7s later another backer joins → **150**, toast "Ama just got down. That's 150. It's happening."
4. 1.1s later the plan is marked hit; 900ms later **It's on.** opens full-screen with confetti.
5. Closing returns to Swipe; the logo badge shows 1; the Inbox gains the It's on item; the Profile shows the Nubian Sq "Soon" stamp.

## Brand

- Colors: ink `#15120F`, cream `#FFFDF8`, paper `#F4EFE6`, accent orange `#FF5A1F`, accent text `#C2410C`, tint `#FFE4D6` / `#8A2E0B`.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 700/800 (display), Geist 400–700 (UI), Geist Mono 500/600 (UPPERCASE labels).
- Surfaces: warm orange radial-gradient background on every tab; cream ticket cards and sheets; frosted glass chips and bars.
- Art: orange fields with black doodle line drawings that depict each plan's theme.
